Jarvis orb: four voxel ideas

None of these bounce or sway. He stays in one spot and turns slowly; only the blocks move. The voice here is made up, so "Speaking" and "Listening" just show how the blocks would react. Click one to see it large.

1 · Pin sphere

Square pins pointing out from the centre, packed tight. They slide in and out, and light leaks out between them.

2 · Voxel ball

Built from cubes on a straight grid, like Minecraft. Whole columns of cubes step in and out one cube at a time.

3 · Plated core

A glowing core of blocks inside a shell of floating plates. The plates lift away and the core pulses with his voice.

4 · Ring stack

Rings of blocks stacked into a sphere. Each ring turns on its own and the blocks pump like a sound meter.
