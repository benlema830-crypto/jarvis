Jarvis orb: four takes on the ring stack

All four are rings of blocks stacked into a sphere, each ring turning on its own. He stays in one spot; only the rings and blocks move. The voice here is made up, so "Speaking" and "Listening" just show how the blocks would react. Click one to see it large.

A · Turbine

Slim angled fins instead of square blocks, with a lit core behind them. The rings spin up as he talks.

B · Reactor

Chunky armour blocks, split across the middle. The halves open to show a bright core and a ring that spikes with his voice.

C · Meter

Black glass columns. Only the part that sticks out lights up, going white at the top, with caps that hang at the peak and drop.

D · Disc stack

Thin toothed discs with light between them. Each whole disc grows and shrinks, so his voice shows in the outline.
